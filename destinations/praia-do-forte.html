Praia do Forte

Sea turtles, natural pools and easy luxury an hour from Salvador

Praia do Forte is the easiest beach escape from Salvador’s airport: a charming pedestrian village of boutiques and restaurants, warm natural pools on the reef, and the headquarters of Projeto TAMAR, Brazil’s celebrated sea-turtle conservation program. It is ideal for families and first-time visitors.

Projeto TAMAR

Brazil’s national sea-turtle program protects nesting beaches along the coast, and its Praia do Forte visitor center lets you see turtles up close, learn about the five species that nest in Brazil and watch feeding times in the afternoon. Tickets were around R$50 (~US$8.5) for adults in 2025 (with family passes); opening days and hours vary by season, so check tamar.org.br and buy online. From roughly September to March you may see nests and hatchlings on the beaches.

Natural pools and beaches

At low tide the reef in front of the village forms warm, shallow pools full of fish — the most famous is Papa-Gente, reached on a short raft trip with local operators. Check the tide table and bring a mask. The long beaches north and south of the village are quieter and lined with palms.

Castelo Garcia d’Ávila and nature

The ruins of a 16th-century fortified house, one of the oldest Portuguese buildings in Brazil, stand on a hill above the village. Nearby reserves offer walks through restinga forest, and humpback whales pass the coast from roughly July to October — boat trips leave from the village.

The village and food

The pedestrian main street, Alameda do Sol, is lined with boutiques, ice-cream shops and restaurants serving moqueca, grilled fish and Bahian dishes. Evenings are relaxed and family-friendly, and the little fishermen’s church on the beach is lovely at sunset.

Where to stay

Choose between big all-inclusive beach resorts just outside the village — popular with families — and smaller pousadas within walking distance of the main street. Book ahead for Christmas, New Year, Carnival and July.

Expect 1.5–3× higher rates over Christmas, New Year and Carnival.

Getting there

Praia do Forte is about 60 km north of Salvador airport, around an hour by car, transfer or regional bus along the Estrada do Coco / Linha Verde coast road (about 1.5 hours from central Salvador). The village center is pedestrian-only.

Praia do Forte FAQ

How far is Praia do Forte from Salvador airport?

About 60 km (40 miles), usually around an hour by car or transfer along the coast road.

How much is Projeto TAMAR in Praia do Forte?

Around R$50 (~US$8.5) for adults in 2025, with family passes available. Check tamar.org.br for current prices and opening days.
