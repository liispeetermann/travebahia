Abrolhos

Humpback whales and Brazil’s first marine park

Created in 1983, Abrolhos was Brazil’s first marine national park and is the most important breeding ground for humpback whales in the South Atlantic. Between July and November thousands of whales arrive from Antarctica to mate and give birth in its warm, shallow waters.

Whale watching

Day boats leave from the municipal pier in Caravelas. Shared trips cost around R$300 (~US$51) per person (children under about 5 often free), with private charters available. The season runs July to November, with the most whales in August and September. Expect a long day — the archipelago is about 2–3 hours offshore each way — and take seasickness tablets.

The archipelago

Abrolhos is a group of five small volcanic islands about 70 km off the coast, surrounded by the largest and richest coral reefs in the South Atlantic. Charles Darwin stopped here aboard the Beagle in 1832. Only Siriba island can be visited on foot, accompanied by park staff; the lighthouse island of Santa Bárbara is used by the Brazilian Navy. The islands are a nesting site for seabirds such as boobies and frigatebirds.

Getting to Caravelas

Caravelas, the gateway town, is a few hours’ drive from Porto Seguro (BPS) airport. Most visitors spend a night or two in Caravelas or nearby Prado to fit in a full day at sea. Book whale trips in advance during August and September.

Tips for the boat day

  • Take seasickness medication before departure — the open-ocean crossing can be rough.
  • Bring sunscreen, a hat, a windbreaker, water and snacks.
  • A zoom lens or binoculars make a big difference for whales.
  • Keep your expectations flexible: sightings and island landings depend on weather and wind.

Diving and park rules

Diving is excellent year-round, with unique mushroom-shaped coral pillars called chapeirões. Rules are strict: diving only at set sites with a park-trained guide, no fishing, no touching or collecting wildlife, and no gloves or knives. Visitors pay a park fee (higher for foreign visitors) through authorized operators, and trips are canceled in strong winds.

Abrolhos FAQ

When can you see whales in Bahia?

Humpback whales visit Bahia’s coast roughly July to November, peaking in August and September. The best sightings are around Abrolhos, with whale trips also from Praia do Forte, Itacaré and Morro de São Paulo.

How much is a whale-watching trip to Abrolhos?

Shared day trips from Caravelas cost around R$300 (~US$51) per person in 2026, plus the park fee. Private charters cost much more.
