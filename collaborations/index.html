Who we are

We’re Liis and Gleb, the travelers behind TravelBahia. We write in-depth, first-hand guides to Bahia for English-speaking travelers, especially from the United States, covering where to go, where to stay, what to eat and how to travel well. Our guides are built to be found: structured for Google and for AI assistants like ChatGPT and Perplexity, where more and more trips now begin.

Who reads TravelBahia

Travelers who are actively planning a trip to Bahia. They search for where to stay in Salvador and Morro de São Paulo, the best natural pools, island tours, safety tips and itineraries. They are looking for exactly the kind of places and experiences you offer, at the moment they are deciding.

How it works

  1. Say hello. Tell us about your property or experience and what you would like to achieve.
  2. We plan together. We agree on the format, dates, deliverables and how the partnership is labeled.
  3. We experience it. We visit, stay or join the experience ourselves. First-hand is the only way we write.
  4. Your story goes live. A polished feature with our photography, published on TravelBahia and kept up to date.

Our promise to readers

We only feature places and experiences we would genuinely recommend to friends, and we always experience them ourselves first. Every paid or gifted partnership is clearly labeled, and affiliate links are marked as sponsored, in line with U.S. FTC guidelines. That honesty is exactly why readers trust our recommendations, and why a feature on TravelBahia is worth having.
