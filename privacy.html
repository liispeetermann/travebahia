Privacy policy

Last updated November 25, 2026

This policy explains what personal information TravelBahia (“we”, “us”) collects when you visit travelbahia.com, subscribe to our newsletter or contact us, how we use it, who we share it with, and the choices and rights you have. We try to collect as little as possible.

Who we are

TravelBahia is an independent travel guide to Bahia, Brazil, run by Liis and Gleb. We are the controller of the personal information described here. Contact us about anything in this policy at hello@travelbahia.com.

Information we collect

When you browse the site

  • Analytics. We use Google Analytics 4 to understand which guides are read and how visitors find us. It collects information such as the pages you view, how long you stay, the site that referred you, your approximate location (city or region, derived from your IP address), device, browser and operating system, and a randomly generated identifier stored in a cookie. Google Analytics 4 does not log or store full IP addresses. We do not use Google Analytics for advertising, and we have not enabled Google signals or ad personalization.
  • Server logs. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub automatically records technical information such as your IP address, browser and the pages requested, for security and to keep the service running. See GitHub’s privacy statement.
  • Embedded content. Some photos load from Unsplash’s and Pexels’ image servers, and our fonts load from Google Fonts. When your browser fetches these files, those providers receive your IP address and browser details. They do not receive anything else from us.

When you subscribe to our newsletter

If you sign up for The Bahia Letter, we collect your email address. Our email provider, MailerLite, also records the date and time you subscribed, your IP address and, when you receive our emails, whether you opened them or clicked a link. We use this only to send the newsletter and to understand which stories readers find useful.

When you email us

If you write to us, we receive your email address, your name if you include it, and whatever you choose to tell us. We use it only to reply and to handle your request, including collaboration enquiries.

When you click a booking or affiliate link

Some links go to booking sites or partners. If you click one, that site may set its own cookies to record that you came from TravelBahia, so we can earn a commission if you book. That information is collected by the partner under its own privacy policy; we only receive aggregated reports (for example, the number of bookings), not who made them.

We do not knowingly collect sensitive information, and we never ask for payment details.

Cookies

We use only a small number of cookies:

  • _ga and _ga_* (Google Analytics) — distinguish visitors and sessions so we can count visits. They last up to 2 years.
  • Partner cookies — set by booking or affiliate sites only after you click through to them.

We do not use cookies for advertising. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings at any time, or opt out of Google Analytics on every site with the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on. The site works fully without cookies.

How we use your information, and our legal bases

  • To run and improve the site (analytics and server logs) — our legitimate interest in keeping the site secure and understanding what readers find useful, and your consent where the law requires it for analytics cookies.
  • To send the newsletter — your consent, which you can withdraw at any time with the unsubscribe link in every email.
  • To answer your messages and manage collaborations — our legitimate interest in responding to you, or taking steps toward an agreement you ask about.
  • To meet legal obligations, such as keeping records or responding to lawful requests.

We do not sell your personal information, we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising, and we do not use it for automated decisions that affect you.

Who we share it with

We share personal information only with service providers that help us run the site, each bound by its own data protection terms:

  • Google (Google Analytics, Google Fonts) — privacy policy
  • MailerLite (newsletter) — privacy policy
  • GitHub (website hosting) — privacy statement
  • Unsplash and Pexels (photo delivery) — Unsplash privacy policy, Pexels privacy policy
  • Our email provider, for messages you send us.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it, or to protect our rights or someone’s safety.

International transfers

Our providers may process data in the United States, the European Union and other countries. Where data from the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland is transferred abroad, they rely on safeguards such as the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework or Standard Contractual Clauses.

How long we keep it

  • Google Analytics data: 14 months, then deleted automatically.
  • Newsletter: for as long as you are subscribed. If you unsubscribe, we keep your email address on a suppression list only so we never email you again.
  • Emails: as long as needed to handle your request, and normally no more than 2 years after our last contact (longer for collaboration agreements, where records are needed).

Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access the personal information we hold about you, correct it, delete it, receive a copy in a portable format, object to or restrict how we use it, and withdraw consent at any time. This includes rights under the EU and UK GDPR and U.S. state privacy laws such as California’s (CCPA/CPRA). To use any of these rights, email hello@travelbahia.com. We will respond within 30 days (45 days where U.S. state law allows) and won’t treat you differently for using your rights. We may need to confirm your identity first. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

Unsubscribing: every newsletter includes a one-click unsubscribe link. Global Privacy Control: we treat a GPC signal as a request to opt out of any “sale” or “sharing”; as described above, we don’t do either.

Children

TravelBahia is written for adults planning travel. We don’t knowingly collect personal information from children under 16. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

The site is served over HTTPS, and we use reputable providers with strong security practices. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to protect your information and limit who can access it.

Links to other sites

Our guides link to official sources, booking sites and other websites. We aren’t responsible for their privacy practices, so please read their policies.

Changes to this policy

We will update this page when our practices change and revise the date at the top. If changes are significant, we will say so on the site or in the newsletter.

Contact

Questions or requests: hello@travelbahia.com.
