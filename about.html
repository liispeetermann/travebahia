Hi, we’re Liis & Gleb

Two travelers who fell for Bahia — its beaches, its food and its rhythm.

Photo: @davidemrich / Unsplash

Who we are

We’re Liis and Gleb — travelers drawn to warm seas, wild jungle and places with soul. Bahia keeps pulling us back with its glass-clear water, rainforest rolling down to the sand, and a rhythm you feel in every street.

Bahia is the part of Brazil we keep returning to — from the cobbled streets of Salvador to the island beaches of Morro de São Paulo. We came for the sea and fell for everything else: turquoise water warm enough to stay in for hours, jungle-covered hills rolling straight down to the sand, and tropical flowers in every garden. We love beautiful, refined stays as much as charming local guesthouses with soul, and we cook often — Brazil’s seafood, beef and tropical fruit are some of the best we have ever tasted.

What stayed with us most is Brazil’s unique, artistic beauty. If you’re used to very polished neighborhoods, the streets might surprise you at first — houses and façades look a bit worn. But they are warm, colorful and full of soul, with plenty of local places for every taste. In Bahia there are drummers on the streets every day, and the performances are amazing.

A few moments from our travels

What we loved most

  • The water. Clear, warm and endlessly blue — natural pools at low tide, reefs alive with fish, and beaches where the sea turns from emerald to turquoise.
  • The jungle. Atlantic rainforest spilling down to the coast, driftwood beaches, red tropical leaves and hibiscus in every garden.
  • The light. Golden evenings over the bay, a caipirinha in hand, with the jungle below and the ocean beyond.
  • The flavors. Shrimp served in a pineapple, ripe mango, the freshest seafood — and cooking with it ourselves.
  • The soul. Drummers in the streets, colorful colonial façades and a warmth that is hard to put into words.
