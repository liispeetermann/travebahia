Best Time to Visit Bahia, Brazil: A Month-by-Month Guide

Quick answer

For beaches and sunshine, visit Bahia between September and March. April to July is the coastal rainy season — April and May are the wettest, though rain often comes in short bursts. Salvador’s Carnival 2027 runs around February 4–10, humpback whales visit from July to November, and Chapada Diamantina is best for hiking in the dry season from April to September. The sea stays warm all year, around 26–28°C (79–82°F).

  • September–November: sunny, fewer crowds, whales offshore
  • December–March: peak summer, festivals, highest prices
  • April–July: rainiest on the coast, cheapest, greenest
  • Carnival 2027: around February 4–10 in Salvador
  • Whales: July–November; Chapada hiking: April–September

Bahia’s seasons at a glance

Bahia sits close to the equator, so it’s warm all year: daytime highs on the coast are usually 27–31°C (81–88°F) and the sea ranges from about 26°C in August–September to 28°C in February. Seasons are about rain, not temperature. Salvador’s rainiest month is April (around 285 mm on average), followed by May, while January and October are among the driest. The Northern Hemisphere winter is Bahia’s summer — a perfect escape from U.S. cold.

Month by month

  • January: hot, sunny high season. The Lavagem do Bonfim — the ritual washing of the Bonfim church steps — falls on the second Thursday of January (January 14, 2027).
  • February: the Festa de Iemanjá on February 2 in Rio Vermelho, then Carnival. In 2027 the pre-Carnival begins in late January and the official Carnival runs around February 4–10. Book months ahead.
  • March: still hot summer; crowds thin after Carnival.
  • April–May: the wettest months on the coast — lush, quiet and cheapest. Easter is busy. Dry and clear in Chapada Diamantina.
  • June: São João (June 23–24) is the Northeast’s biggest festival after Carnival, with forró, bonfires and corn dishes, especially in interior towns.
  • July–August: mild and mixed weather; Brazilian school holidays in July make it busy. Humpback whales arrive. Best winds for kitesurfing.
  • September–November: our favorite — sunny, breezy, less crowded, whales offshore and good prices.
  • December: summer begins. New Year’s Eve in Salvador, Morro and Trancoso is legendary — and the most expensive week of the year.

Festivals worth planning around

  • Lavagem do Bonfim (second Thursday of January): a procession of Baianas in white to the Bonfim church.
  • Festa de Iemanjá (February 2): offerings to the sea goddess on the beach in Rio Vermelho.
  • Carnival (February/March): Salvador’s street Carnival is one of the largest on Earth.
  • São João (late June): forró and bonfires across the interior.
  • Independence of Bahia (July 2): parades through Salvador’s historic center.

Whale season

Humpback whales migrate from Antarctica to breed off southern Bahia from July to November, with the biggest numbers in August and September. Abrolhos is the hotspot, but you can also see them from Praia do Forte, Itacaré and Morro de São Paulo. See our Abrolhos guide.

Chapada Diamantina’s seasons

Inland, the pattern is different. The dry season (April–September) is best for hiking, clear views and the famous blue light in Poço Encantado (roughly April–September, late morning). September is the driest month. The wet season (November–March) brings fuller waterfalls but also storms and the risk of flash floods. Nights get cold — down to around 10°C — in June to August.

Tides matter more than you think

Many of Bahia’s natural pools — Taipu de Fora, Moreré, Garapuá, Morro’s Quarta Praia — only appear at low tide and are best on very low tides near full and new moons. Check a tide table (tábua de marés) before you plan pool days.

Crowds and prices

Prices peak from mid-December through Carnival, and again in July and on long weekends. For the best balance of weather, value and space, aim for September to November or early March. Book Carnival and New Year’s Eve accommodation six months or more ahead.

Frequently asked questions

What is the rainy season in Salvador?

Roughly April to July, with April and May the wettest months. Rain often falls in heavy but short bursts.

When is Carnival in Salvador in 2027?

Salvador’s official Carnival 2027 runs around February 4–10, with pre-Carnival events starting in late January. Dates change every year with Easter.

What is the best month to visit Bahia?

September to November offers sunny, breezy weather, fewer crowds and whales offshore. December to March is hotter and busier with festivals.

How warm is the sea in Bahia?

Warm all year: about 26°C (79°F) in August and September and up to about 28°C (82°F) in February.
