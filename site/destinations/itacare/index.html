Itacaré

Surf beaches tucked into the Atlantic rainforest

Photo: @gabrielnakamura / Unsplash

Best timeSep–Mar; surf all year
Ideal stay3–4 days
Getting thereIOS – Ilhéus (about 1 hour)
VibeSurf, rainforest, yoga, adventure

On this page

  1. Best beaches
  2. Beyond the beach
  3. FAQ

Itacaré is where the Atlantic rainforest crashes straight into the surf. A laid-back town with a backpacker-meets-boutique vibe, it is surrounded by jungle-trail beaches, waterfalls and the cacao farms that made southern Bahia famous.

  • Surf lessons
  • Prainha jungle trail
  • Cacao farm tour
  • Rafting
  • Waterfalls

Best beaches

  • Tiririca: the town surf beach and contest spot.
  • Prainha: reached by a jungle trail — arguably the prettiest beach in the region.
  • Havaizinho, Engenhoca & Itacarezinho: wild, palm-backed beaches off the coastal road.

Beyond the beach

Raft the Rio de Contas, swim under the Tijuípe waterfall, tour an organic cacao farm and taste bean-to-bar chocolate, or join a surf lesson — the town has excellent schools for beginners.

Itacaré in pictures

Itacaré FAQ

Is Itacaré good for beginner surfers?

Yes. Several beaches have mellow waves on smaller days and the town has many surf schools offering lessons in English.
