Trancoso

Bohemian chic on a clifftop square above endless beaches

Photo: @limamauro23 / Unsplash

Best timeSep–Mar; New Year is peak
Ideal stay3–5 days
Getting thereBPS – Porto Seguro (about 1 hour)
VibeBoutique, design, romance

On this page

  1. Highlights
  2. FAQ

Trancoso is southern Bahia at its most stylish. Life centers on the Quadrado, a grassy square of colorful fishermen’s houses turned boutiques and restaurants, with a little white church at its end and a cliff dropping to the beach below.

  • Quadrado square
  • Praia do Espelho
  • Caraíva
  • Boutique hotels

Highlights

  • The Quadrado at dusk when lanterns light up the square.
  • Praia do Espelho: one of Brazil’s most beautiful beaches, with reef pools and colored cliffs.
  • Caraíva: a car-free river village reached by canoe, perfect for a day or two.

Trancoso in pictures

Trancoso FAQ

How do you get to Trancoso?

Fly to Porto Seguro (BPS), then drive about an hour, or take the ferry across the river to Arraial d’Ajuda and continue by road.
