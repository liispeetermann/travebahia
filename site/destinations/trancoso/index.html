Trancoso

Bohemian chic on a clifftop square above endless beaches

Trancoso is southern Bahia at its most stylish. Life centers on the Quadrado, a long grassy square of colorful fishermen’s houses turned boutiques and restaurants, with the little white church of São João Batista at its end and a cliff dropping to the beach below.

The Quadrado

Cars can’t drive on the Quadrado, which makes it a magical place to wander, especially at dusk when lanterns light up the trees and restaurants set tables on the grass. Steps lead down the cliff to the beaches below.

Praia do Espelho

One of Brazil’s most beautiful beaches, with reef pools at low tide and red-and-ochre cliffs. It’s about 40 minutes’ drive south of Trancoso, much of it on potholed dirt road; taxis charge roughly R$350–400 for a round trip for up to four. Expect a parking fee and steep steps cut into the cliff. Go at low tide.

Caraíva

A car-free river village about an hour further south on dirt roads. You cross the river by canoe (a few reais) into sandy lanes, forró bars and a beach where the river meets the sea. Worth a day — or two.

Beaches around Trancoso

  • Praia dos Nativos and Praia dos Coqueiros: the beaches right below the Quadrado, with beach clubs and barracas.
  • Rio Verde: where a small river meets the sea, calm and good for families.
  • Itapororoca: long and quiet, backed by cliffs and luxury hideaways.
  • Praia do Espelho and Curuípe: reef pools and colored cliffs further south.

Arraial d’Ajuda

Between Porto Seguro and Trancoso, Arraial d’Ajuda is a livelier hilltop village with a pedestrian lane of bars and shops, beautiful beaches such as Pitinga and Mucugê, and a big water park. It’s a good-value alternative base.

When to go and where to stay

September to March is the main season. Christmas and New Year are Trancoso’s peak — prices soar and you need to book many months ahead. March to June and August to November are calmer and better value. Stay around the Quadrado to walk to restaurants, or by the beaches for quiet.

Getting there

Fly to Porto Seguro (BPS), then drive about an hour via the ferry to Arraial d’Ajuda and the coast road. Ask taxi drivers on the Quadrado about current dirt-road conditions before driving to Espelho or Caraíva; a higher-clearance car helps.

Trancoso FAQ

How do you get to Trancoso?

Fly to Porto Seguro (BPS), then drive about an hour, crossing by ferry to Arraial d’Ajuda and continuing on the coast road.

How far is Praia do Espelho from Trancoso?

About 24–26 km, roughly 40 minutes by car, much of it on dirt road.
