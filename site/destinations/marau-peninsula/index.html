Maraú Peninsula

Taipu de Fora’s reef pools and a lagoon-laced wilderness

Photo: @jeisen / Unsplash

Best timeSep–Mar; low tide days
Ideal stay3–4 days
Getting thereIOS or SSA + boat from Camamu

Remote and gloriously undeveloped, the Maraú Peninsula is reached by boat across the Camamu Bay to Barra Grande. Its star is Taipu de Fora, a vast reef that becomes a natural aquarium at low tide.

Maraú Peninsula FAQ

How do I get to Barra Grande?

Most travelers take a speedboat from Camamu (reachable by road from Salvador or Ilhéus), about 30–45 minutes across the bay. There is also a dirt road from Itacaré.
