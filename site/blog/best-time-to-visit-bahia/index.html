Best Time to Visit Bahia, Brazil: A Month-by-Month Guide

Planning By Liis & Gleb Updated Nov 24, 2026 7 min read

  2. Month by month

Quick answer

The best time for Bahia’s beaches is September through March, when it’s sunny and warm. April to July is the coastal rainy season (May is wettest), though showers are often short. Carnival is in February or early March, humpback whales visit July to November, and Chapada Diamantina is best for hiking April to October.

  • Sep–Nov: sunny, fewer crowds, great value
  • Dec–Mar: peak summer, festivals, highest prices
  • Apr–Jul: rainier, greener, cheapest
  • Jul–Nov: whale season on the coast

Bahia’s seasons at a glance

Bahia sits close to the equator, so it’s warm all year — roughly 75–90°F on the coast. Seasons are about rain, not temperature. The Northern Hemisphere winter is Bahia’s summer, which makes it a perfect escape from U.S. cold.

Month by month

  • January: hot, sunny high season. The Lavagem do Bonfim festival fills Salvador’s streets.
  • February: the Iemanjá festival on Feb 2 and, most years, Carnival — the biggest street party on Earth. Book months ahead.
  • March: still summer, crowds thin after Carnival.
  • April–June: the rainy months on the coast; lush, quiet and cheaper. June’s São João festivals are a Northeast highlight. Dry and clear in Chapada.
  • July–August: mild, mixed weather; Brazilian school holidays in July. Whales arrive.
  • September–November: our favorite: sunny, breezy, less crowded, whales offshore.
  • December: summer begins; New Year’s Eve in Trancoso and Salvador is legendary.

Tides matter more than you think

Many of Bahia’s famous natural pools — Taipu de Fora, Moreré, Garapuá, Morro’s Quarta Praia — only appear at low tide. Check a tide table (tábua de marés) and plan beach days around full and new moons, when low tides are lowest.

Frequently asked questions

What is the rainy season in Salvador?

Roughly April to July, with May typically the wettest month. Rain often falls in heavy but short bursts.

When is Carnival in Salvador?

Carnival happens in February or early March, the days before Ash Wednesday. Dates change yearly with Easter.
