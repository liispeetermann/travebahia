How to Get from Salvador to Morro de São Paulo (Catamaran, Ferry or Flight)

Getting There By Liis & Gleb Updated Nov 24, 2026 6 min read

Quick answer

You have three options: the direct catamaran from Salvador’s Terminal Náutico (about 2–2.5 hours, can be rough), the semi-terrestre route of ferry + road + speedboat via Valença (about 3.5–4 hours, calmer), or a small plane (about 25 minutes, priciest). If you get seasick, choose semi-terrestre or fly.

  • Catamaran = fastest by sea, rough on windy days
  • Semi-terrestre = longer but sheltered
  • Flights = 25 minutes, luggage limits apply
  • Morning departures have the calmest seas

Option 1: Catamaran (direct)

Several companies run catamarans from the Terminal Náutico, next to the Mercado Modelo, straight to Morro’s pier. The crossing takes around 2 to 2.5 hours across open ocean. On windy days it’s bumpy — take motion-sickness medicine 30 minutes before boarding, sit at the back, and look at the horizon. Buy tickets a day ahead in high season.

Option 2: Semi-terrestre (ferry + road + boat)

Take the ferry (ferry-boat) or a small launch from Salvador to Itaparica island (Bom Despacho), then a van or bus to Valença, and finally a speedboat down the river and bay to Morro. Total time is around 3.5–4 hours, but the water sections are sheltered. Many agencies sell the whole route as one combined ticket.

Option 3: Fly

Small air-taxi planes connect Salvador airport to Morro de São Paulo’s airstrip in about 25 minutes, with spectacular views. It’s the best choice if you land in Salvador and want to be on the beach the same afternoon. Check baggage weight limits — they are strict.

Arriving in Morro

There are no cars on the island. Porters with wheelbarrows wait at the pier and will carry your luggage to your pousada for a small fee — agree on the price first. A municipal tourism fee is charged on arrival; keep the receipt.

Frequently asked questions

How long is the catamaran from Salvador to Morro de São Paulo?

About 2 to 2.5 hours, depending on sea conditions.

What is the best way to get to Morro de São Paulo if I get seasick?

Take the semi-terrestre route via Itaparica and Valença — the boat sections are in sheltered water — or fly in about 25 minutes.
