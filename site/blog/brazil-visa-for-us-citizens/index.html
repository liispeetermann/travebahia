Brazil Visa for U.S. Citizens: The e-Visa Explained (2026)

Planning By Liis & Gleb Updated Nov 24, 2026 5 min read

Quick answer

Yes. Since April 10, 2025, U.S. citizens need a Brazilian electronic visa (e-visa) for tourism. You apply online through Brazil’s official e-visa portal, pay the fee, upload a passport scan, photo and supporting documents, and typically receive it by email. Apply at least several weeks before departure and always verify current rules with the Brazilian consulate.

  • Apply only through the official government portal
  • Passport valid at least 6 months is strongly advised
  • Have proof of onward travel and accommodation ready
  • Print a copy of the e-visa for check-in

Who needs it

Brazil reinstated visa requirements for U.S., Canadian and Australian citizens on April 10, 2025. The e-visa is for tourism, business visits and visiting family, not for work or study.

How to apply

  1. Go to the official Brazilian e-visa portal linked from the Brazilian Ministry of Foreign Affairs or your local consulate’s website.
  2. Fill in the application and upload a passport bio-page scan and a recent photo.
  3. Add supporting documents (such as a bank statement, and sometimes flight and hotel bookings).
  4. Pay the fee by card and wait for approval by email.

Beware of look-alike websites

Many third-party sites charge large markups to “process” your application. Make sure the URL belongs to the Brazilian government.

Rules change — double-check

Fees, validity and processing times can change. Treat this page as a starting point and confirm on the official portal and the U.S. State Department’s Brazil page before you book.

Frequently asked questions

Do U.S. citizens need a visa for Brazil in 2026?

Yes. Since April 10, 2025, Americans need an electronic visa (e-visa) for tourist visits to Brazil, applied for online before travel.

How long does the Brazil e-visa take?

Processing is often a few business days, but it can take longer. Apply at least several weeks before your trip.
