Brazil Visa for U.S. Citizens: The e-Visa Explained (2026)

Quick answer

Yes. Since April 10, 2025, U.S. citizens need a Brazilian e-visa to visit for tourism. Apply online through the official portal operated by VFS Global for the Brazilian government, pay the US$80.90 fee, upload your passport, a photo and supporting documents, and receive the visa by email as a PDF. Allow up to 10 business days — apply two to three weeks before travel. Stays are limited to 90 days at a time.

  • Mandatory for U.S. tourists since April 10, 2025
  • Fee: US$80.90, paid online, non-refundable
  • Apply only on the official VFS Global e-visa portal
  • Allow up to 10 business days; apply 2–3 weeks ahead
  • Max 90 days per stay, 180 days per 12 months

Who needs it

Brazil reinstated visa requirements for citizens of the United States, Canada and Australia on April 10, 2025, on the principle of reciprocity (Americans require Brazilians to obtain U.S. visas). The e-visa covers tourism, business visits, visiting family and attending events. It does not allow work or study.

What it costs and how long it lasts

  • Fee: US$80.90 (US$80 consular fee plus a small processing fee), paid by card online. It’s non-refundable, even if your application is refused.
  • Validity: multiple entries, typically issued for up to 10 years for U.S. citizens — check the validity printed on your visa.
  • Length of stay: up to 90 days per visit, and no more than 180 days in any 12-month period. Extensions are handled by Brazil’s Federal Police while you’re in the country.

Documents you need

  • A passport valid for the whole stay (six months beyond arrival is the safe standard), signed, with blank pages
  • A recent passport-style photo (digital)
  • A scan of your passport bio page
  • Proof of financial means, such as a recent bank statement
  • Travel details — a flight itinerary or reservation, and where you’ll stay

How to apply, step by step

  1. Go to the official Brazil e-visa portal operated by VFS Global (linked from the Brazilian Ministry of Foreign Affairs and Brazilian consulate websites).
  2. Create an account and fill in the application.
  3. Upload your photo, passport scan and supporting documents.
  4. Pay the fee by card.
  5. Wait for the decision by email — the approved visa arrives as a PDF.

Processing time

Many applications are approved within a few business days, but official guidance allows up to 10 business days, and processing can be slower around Carnival and the December holidays. Apply at least two to three weeks before your trip — and before you book non-refundable plans if your trip is soon.

Beware of look-alike websites

Many third-party sites charge large markups to “process” your application, and some look official. Make sure you’re on the government-authorized VFS Global portal before entering card details. If a site charges much more than US$80.90, it’s an intermediary.

At the border

Print a copy of your e-visa and keep the PDF on your phone. Airlines may ask to see it at check-in. Have your return or onward ticket and accommodation details ready in case immigration asks.

Rules change — double-check

Brazil’s visa policy for Americans has changed several times in recent years, and Congress has debated reversing it. Treat this page as a starting point and confirm on the official portal and the U.S. State Department’s Brazil page before you book.

Frequently asked questions

Do U.S. citizens need a visa for Brazil in 2026?

Yes. Since April 10, 2025, Americans need an electronic visa (e-visa) for tourist visits to Brazil, applied for online before travel.

How much is the Brazil e-visa for Americans?

US$80.90, paid online by card. The fee is non-refundable.

How long does the Brazil e-visa take?

Often a few business days, but allow up to 10 business days, and longer around Carnival and holidays. Apply two to three weeks before your trip.

How long can I stay in Brazil on an e-visa?

Up to 90 days per visit and no more than 180 days in any 12-month period.
