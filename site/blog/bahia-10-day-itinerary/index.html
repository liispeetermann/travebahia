The Perfect 10-Day Bahia Itinerary for First-Time Visitors

Quick answer

Spend days 1–3 in Salvador (Pelourinho, Barra, Rio Vermelho, Bonfim), days 4–7 in Morro de São Paulo with the full-day island tour to Garapuá, Moreré and Boipeba, then choose days 8–10 in Chapada Diamantina for hiking and blue caves or Itacaré for surf and rainforest. Fly in and out of Salvador (SSA), take the morning catamaran, and leave a buffer before your flight home.

  • Fly into and out of Salvador (SSA)
  • Salvador → Morro: catamaran ~2.5 h (~R$150–175) + R$70 island fee
  • Morro island tour: ~R$350, check the tide
  • Chapada for hiking; Itacaré for surf and jungle beaches
  • Leave 6–8 hours from Morro to an international flight

Before you go

  • Apply for the Brazil e-visa at least 2–3 weeks ahead.
  • Most U.S. travelers connect via São Paulo, Rio, Miami or Panama City to Salvador.
  • Book Morro catamarans and Chapada transport ahead in high season.
  • Bring some cash in small bills for islands and markets.

Day 1: Arrive in Salvador

Check into Barra, the easiest base. Walk the seafront, swim at Porto da Barra, and watch sunset at the Farol da Barra lighthouse — locals applaud as the sun drops into the bay. The lighthouse’s nautical museum costs R$20. Dinner and acarajé in Rio Vermelho. Salvador guide →

Day 2: The historic center

Spend the morning in the Pelourinho: the Catedral Basílica on Terreiro de Jesus (R$10), the blue Igreja do Rosário dos Pretos, the Casa do Carnaval and the Fundação Casa de Jorge Amado. Note that the gilded São Francisco church is closed for restoration until around 2029. Ride the Elevador Lacerda (R$1) down to the Mercado Modelo, then end at the Solar do Unhão / MAM for sunset over the bay. In the evening, book the Balé Folclórico da Bahia (Mon, Wed and Fri).

Day 3: Bonfim, Ribeira and the Dique

Visit the Igreja do Senhor do Bonfim and tie a ribbon to the railings, then head to Ribeira for ice cream by the water. In the afternoon, see the Orixá sculptures floating on the Dique do Tororó. Pack for the islands.

Day 4: Salvador → Morro de São Paulo

Take the morning catamaran from the Terminal Náutico (about 2.5 hours, around R$150–175) — or the semi-terrestre route if you get seasick. Pay the R$70 TUPA fee on arrival, let a porter wheel your bags up the hill, and spend the afternoon on Segunda Praia. Sunset from the Forte de Tapirandu. Transport guide →

Days 5–7: Morro, Boipeba and the pools

  • Day 5: The full-day volta à ilha boat tour (about 8 hours, around R$350) with stops at Garapuá’s natural pools, Moreré, Boipeba and the colonial town of Cairu. Lunch is usually extra.
  • Day 6: Walk the beaches from Terceira to Quarta Praia at low tide and float in the pools. Try the zip line from the fort over Primeira Praia.
  • Day 7: Clay baths and sunset at Gamboa, or a 4x4 trip to Garapuá. Optional sunset catamaran.

Days 8–10, Option A: Chapada Diamantina

Return to Salvador on the morning boat and take the overnight Rápido Federal bus to Lençóis (about 6–7 hours, book leito), or fly on Thursday or Sunday. Then: Pai Inácio sunset and Ribeirão do Meio (day 8), the top of Cachoeira da Fumaça (day 9), and Poço Azul and Pratinha (day 10). Return to Salvador overnight. Chapada guide →

Days 8–10, Option B: Itacaré

Continue south to Itacaré (via Valença and the coast road, or back through Salvador and a flight to Ilhéus, about an hour from Itacaré). Take a surf lesson at Engenhoca or Ribeira, hike the Prainha trail and the four-beaches trail, tour a cacao farm and paddle to the Cleandro waterfall. Fly home via Ilhéus or Salvador. Rough budget

For two people traveling mid-range, expect roughly US$150–300 per day including a comfortable pousada, meals, tours and transport — more over Christmas, New Year and Carnival. Island tours, Chapada guides and domestic flights are the biggest extras.

Frequently asked questions

Is 10 days enough for Bahia?

Ten days is enough for Salvador, Morro de São Paulo and one more region such as Chapada Diamantina or Itacaré at a comfortable pace.

Should I choose Chapada Diamantina or Itacaré?

Choose Chapada Diamantina for hiking, waterfalls and blue caves, and Itacaré for surfing, rainforest-backed beaches and a relaxed beach-town vibe.
