The Perfect 10-Day Bahia Itinerary for First-Time Visitors

Quick answer

Spend days 1–3 in Salvador (Pelourinho, Barra, Rio Vermelho), days 4–7 in Morro de São Paulo with the island boat tour to Boipeba and Garapuá, then choose days 8–10 in Chapada Diamantina for hiking or Itacaré for surf and rainforest before flying home from Salvador.

  • Fly into and out of Salvador (SSA)
  • Take the morning catamaran to Morro
  • Pick Chapada for hiking, Itacaré for surf
  • Leave a buffer day before your flight home

Days 1–3: Salvador

Day 1: settle into Barra, sunset at Farol da Barra. Day 2: the Pelourinho, São Francisco church, Elevador Lacerda and Mercado Modelo. Day 3: Bonfim church in the morning, acarajé and nightlife in Rio Vermelho. Salvador guide →

Days 4–7: Morro de São Paulo & Boipeba

Catamaran or fly to Morro. Spend a day between Segunda and Terceira Praia, a day on the full island boat tour (Garapuá, Moreré pools, Boipeba), a day walking to Quarta Praia at low tide, and sunset at the lighthouse. Morro guide →

Days 8–10, Option A: Chapada Diamantina

Return to Salvador and head inland to Lençóis for Morro do Pai Inácio, Cachoeira da Fumaça and the blue caves. Chapada guide →

Days 8–10, Option B: Itacaré

Continue south to Itacaré for surf lessons, the Prainha trail and a cacao farm tour; fly home via Ilhéus. Itacaré guide →

Frequently asked questions

Is 10 days enough for Bahia?

Ten days is enough for Salvador, Morro de São Paulo and one more region like Chapada Diamantina or Itacaré at a comfortable pace.
