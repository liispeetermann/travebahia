Chapada Diamantina Hiking Guide: Waterfalls, Blue Caves & the Vale do Pati

Quick answer

Base yourself in Lençóis for 4–7 days. Don’t miss sunset on Morro do Pai Inácio, the top of Cachoeira da Fumaça, the blue-lit caves of Poço Azul and Poço Encantado, and — if you have time — the 3–5 day Vale do Pati trek. Hire accredited local guides; April to October is best for hiking.

  • Lençóis is the best base for first-timers
  • Hire a local guide for most trails
  • Blue cave light is best April–September
  • Pack trail shoes, headlamp and quick-dry layers

A 5-day Chapada itinerary

  1. Day 1: Arrive in Lençóis, swim at Ribeirão do Meio, sunset on Morro do Pai Inácio.
  2. Day 2: Hike to the top of Cachoeira da Fumaça.
  3. Day 3: Caves day — Lapa Doce, Pratinha and Poço Azul.
  4. Day 4: Poço Encantado and Cachoeira do Buracão.
  5. Day 5: Serrano rock pools and departure — or start the Vale do Pati trek.

The Vale do Pati trek

Frequently called one of Brazil’s most beautiful treks, the Pati valley is hiked over 3–5 days with nights in simple local family homes that serve home-cooked meals. A guide is essential.

What to pack

  • Broken-in trail shoes and sandals for river crossings
  • Swimsuit and quick-dry towel
  • Headlamp for caves
  • Rain jacket, especially December–March
  • Refillable bottle with filter

Frequently asked questions

How many days do you need in Chapada Diamantina?

At least 4 days for the highlights; 7 days if you want to add the Vale do Pati trek.

Is Chapada Diamantina good for beginners?

Yes. Many highlights, like Morro do Pai Inácio and the caves, are short and accessible; just bring good shoes and hire a guide.
